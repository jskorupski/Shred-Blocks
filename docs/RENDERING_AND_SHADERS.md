On-demand reference for **WebGL2 / Canvas2D rendering**, **Z-layer depth ordering**, **3D block skins & board tray shaders**, **120Hz frame-budget rules**, and the **Title Screen Satin Astrolabe & Relic Awakening FX**. Read this before modifying `src/render/*`, `src/audio/*`, or `src/styles/title-and-a11y.css`.

## 1. 4-Pass WebGL2 Pipeline & 120Hz Frame Budget (`<= 6.5ms`)

[`src/render/renderer-3d.ts`](../src/render/renderer-3d.ts) renders each frame in 4 passes (with parity in [`canvas2d-fallback.ts`](../src/render/canvas2d-fallback.ts)):
1. **Pass 1 (`renderPass1Environment` — [`pass1-courtyard.ts`](../src/render/pass1-courtyard.ts), [`pass1-gates-barriers.ts`](../src/render/pass1-gates-barriers.ts))**:
   - Draws the 3-tier **Stepped Architectural Sanctuary Plinth** (`z = -0.50, -0.34, -0.18`). **Row-Span Merging (`plinthSpans`)**: Never issue per-cell `drawMesh` calls for plinths under sculpted (`L14+`) or multi-room (`L25+`) boards; merge contiguous row spans so Pass 1 stays at `~15–24` draw calls.
   - Renders recessed courtyard tiles, stone pillars, elevator rails, generator housings, crates, and perimeter shredder gates (rollers spin at `1.35 rad/s` idle and `18.0 rad/s` only when shredding or aligned with a dragged block; queued color bars; star emblems; **3D Sculpted Frost Medallions `[❄️ N]`** via `drawBadgeMedallion3D`; shutter bars).
2. **Pass 2 (`renderPass2BarriersAndReticles` — [`pass2-barriers-and-reticles.ts`](../src/render/pass2-barriers-and-reticles.ts), [`pass2-drag-fx.ts`](../src/render/pass2-drag-fx.ts), [`path-trace-ribbon.ts`](../src/render/path-trace-ribbon.ts))**:
   - Draws **3D Sanctuary Portcullis Barriers (`⛩ N`)**, **3D Carved Sanctuary Mystery Vault Lids (`bar.isVault`, `materialStyle = 2`)** with gilded bronze bezels, indigo-violet coffered slabs, horizontal louver ribs, corner bolsters, and `[🔒 N]` medallions (leaving flush `Z = 0.008` brass corner inlays when unsealed), grid shadow reticles, green alignment beams, solver hint chevrons, 3D shockwave rings (`activeShockwaves`), and booster targeting highlights (`ROCKET-AIM-01` row/column reticles appear only when hovered/aimed).
   - **Mid-Drag Origin Ghost (`ORIGIN-GHOST-01`) & Post-Commit Motion-Blur Smear (`MYSTIC-TRACE-01`)**: Renders tray-luminance-adaptive perimeter-aware 4-corner L-brackets (`drawCellOuterCornerBrackets3D` + `expand = 0.016` mat under-halo via `resolveOriginGhostStyle`, raised to `zOffset + 0.015` above `FLOOR_PATHS`, dark slate-ink + ivory under-halo on light trays and obsidian-cushioned pearl on `midnight_arcade`) + `↩` (`BADGE_RETURN`) at the starting anchor when displaced `>= 1` cell (shifting to a tray-adapted cancel frame via `resolveReturnHomeStyle` if dragged back onto origin). Committed moves/shreds spawn a `0.50s` block-colored motion-blur smear (`u_materialStyle = 8`) rendered on a **single 2-triangle bounding quad** (`ENVIRONMENT_MESHES.overlayQuad`, `0` polygon fan lines on curves/diagonals) with immediate full-path span (`headDist = totalLength`, zero mid-board truncation), move-length-adaptive comet-tail narrowing + feathering (`fillTraceUniformWindow`), `alongHalf`-scaled rounded capsule caps (near-circular `~1:1` on diagonals, never squared-off), exact 16-bit polyomino `headCellMask` leading-silhouette masking (`bestShadowD`, so smears strictly trail behind the block and never leak out its leading edge or concave notches even on 1-cell moves of wide bars), CVD/HC color matching, light/dark tray contrast adaptation, and `reducedMotion` suppression.
3. **Pass 3 (`renderPass3Blocks` — [`pass3-block-shells.ts`](../src/render/pass3-block-shells.ts), [`pass3-block-overlays.ts`](../src/render/pass3-block-overlays.ts))**:
   - Renders unlocked 3D polyomino blocks (skipping concealed `isBlockInSealedVault` blocks/welds), hardware clip-plane shred animations (`u_useClipPlane`), multi-layer inner cores, chamfered 3D circular studs (`addCylinderCapToBuilder`), glued solder bridges, **3D beveled side-flange Axis-Lock rails** (`railBeamMesh` tinted with `entry.lipHex` + champagne cap, leaving top studs 100% uncovered), **sculpted 3D badge medallions** (`drawBadgeMedallion3D`), **hollow 3D move-guide frames** (`drawHollowMoveGuideFrame`), and `AX-01` colorblind symbols.
   - **Grabbed Focus**: Lifts by `BLOCK_LIFT_DELTA_Z = 0.46`, scales `1.05x` around geometric center, casts an offset floor shadow, and dims non-grabbed blocks to `0.88x`.
   - **Directional Lighting**: Blends warm sunlit key light (`vec3(1.04, 0.98, 0.92)`) on top faces/stud bevels with cool lavender-indigo shadow fill (`vec3(0.90, 0.88, 1.03)`) on side faces.
4. **Pass 4 (`renderPass4Particles` — [`particle-fx.ts`](../src/render/particle-fx.ts), [`shred-style-fx.ts`](../src/render/shred-style-fx.ts), [`shred-gate-bursts.ts`](../src/render/shred-gate-bursts.ts), [`shaders-particles.ts`](../src/render/shaders-particles.ts), [`shred-style-voices.ts`](../src/audio/shred-style-voices.ts), `SHRED-STYLE-01`, `SHRED-AUDIO-01`, `FX-PACING-01`)**:
   - GPU-instanced 3D particle system (`gl.drawElementsInstanced`, `MAX_PARTICLES = 2048`, `PARTICLE_STRIDE = 15` with `styleTag` in slot `14` and zero fragment `discard` for mobile tile-GPU Early-Z; **`FX-PACING-01`**: keep mid-stage `!isFinalClear` particle lifetimes brisk `<= ~1.3s` so board sightlines clear quickly, reserving `1.85–2.96s` celebratory lingers for `isBoardWon` final clears) with 5 distinct physics/geometry/audio styles (`playStyledShredSoundscape` in [`shred-style-voices.ts`](../src/audio/shred-style-voices.ts), used across both live `playShred` and `playParticlePreview`): `micro_cubes` (`0c`, 3-axis beveled cubes + courtyard bounce + rosewood/stone marimba cascade & staggered tile scatter micro-clicks), `star_confetti` (`250c`, 5-point polar-SDF foil stars + low-gravity S-wave flutter + festive air-popper puff & sparkling celesta/glockenspiel foil-tine bell cascade), `pixel_sparks` (`450c`, velocity-aligned `atan2(vy, vx)` laser streaks + floor ricochet + signature retro `square`-wave arcade laser-zips & 8-bit spark ricochet pings), `soap_bubbles` (`750c`, `1.85x` multi-size analytic ray-sphere rainbow thin-film bubbles with air-cushion drag, looping figure-8 `(X, Y)` thermal eddies, `+1.22` upward buoyancy, brisk `0.88–1.28s` normal-play lifetimes vs. lingering `1.85–2.96s` celebratory final-clear flourishes, and `1.38x` pop swell + gentle wand breath, upward-sweeping liquid-glass resonant bloops & high-Q water-film pops), and `lightning_bolts` (`1200c`, additive velocity-aligned strobing plasma sparks with electromagnetic flutter + stepped-leader jolt, paired with single-quad `u_materialStyle = 9` volumetric `PLASMA_ARCS` gate lightning trees & jaw arc-welder corona flashes featuring 120Hz writhing, asynchronous multi-strike re-pathing, traveling surge pulses, and plasma-cooling dissolve fade at the end of Pass 3 + electrostatic snap, detuned `sawtooth` electric-arc fifths & swept resonant plasma restrike crackles).
   - **Extended Sanctuary Bleed, Gate-Jaw Contact & FX State Reset (`FX-STATE-01..03`)**: `#game-canvas` carries a `1.36×` bleed halo inside `.board-stage` (`computeBoardBleedFraming` keeps 3D board pixels 100% unchanged; `computeEffectiveDpr` caps `min(900 / boardMaxCss, 1024 / canvasMaxCss)` and caches `lastAppliedMaxDpr` to prevent `ResizeObserver` `devicePixelContentBoxSize` `1px` backbuffer ping-pong and preserve `<= 6.5ms` 120Hz budget at 3× mobile DPR). Stationary/dragged blocks and idle gates emit `0` particles prior to collision; gate shreds originate strictly at the shredder jaw (`computeGateShredContactPoint`) with outward-clamped velocity (`outDot >= 0.55`) and pack 10-bit `lifeRatio` in `floor(a_instPosScale.w)` + scale in `fract(a_instPosScale.w)` so `v_fade = lifeFade * edgeFade` eases alpha smoothly to `0.0`. `lastSyncedBoardRef` purges transient FX across every Undo, Restart, Level Load, Mode Reset, and screen switch.
   - **WCAG 2.3.3 Reduced-Motion Gate Seals**: When `reducedMotion === true`, `spawnStyledParticlesImpl` spawns `0` flying projectiles and renders a stationary, non-strobing `REDUCED_MOTION_SEAL` gate emblem (`0.36s` smooth fade) distinct per style across WebGL2 and Canvas2D. All cosmetic randomness must use `fxRandom()` ([`src/core/fx-random.ts`](../src/core/fx-random.ts)), never `Math.random()`.

## 5. Adaptive Quality Governor (`VER-03`), rAF Pacing & Victory Modal FX Budgets

- **Sub-60fps Distress `AUTO` Tiering & LTPO Touch-Cadence Telemetry ([`src/render/quality-governor.ts`](../src/render/quality-governor.ts))**:
  - `AUTO` defaults to `ULTRA_120` (unthrottled vsync, crisp `2.5` DPR, `60` particles) and downgrades to `BALANCED_60` **only on genuine sub-60fps distress**: `>= 20%` of active motion frames stalling `> 22ms` (`sub60StallRatio >= 0.2`) or heavy CPU/GL submission (`p95WorkMs > 11ms`), with exponential upgrade holdoff (`240 .. 1800` ticks). Both `120Hz` (`8.33ms`) and `60Hz` (`16.67ms` Safari / Android finger-up LTPO) count as 100% healthy.
  - `cadenceRing` (`didLastTickSampleCadence`) samples active pointer/drag and high-rate (`< 10.5ms`) intervals separately from finger-up cooldowns and freezes while `<dialog>` modals are open, so reaching for Settings (`#dialog-pause`) on Android Chrome LTPO never decays `120fps (120Hz)` telemetry down to `60`. Synchronous non-rAF draws (`trackRafDelta = false`) never spoof `FPS`; `onHoverChanged` / `onDragFrame` defer live frame draws to the single vsync `rAF` pass unless `frozenTimeSec !== null`.
- **`BALANCED_60` Instantaneous Sub-Frame Pacing, Outcome Choreography (`OUTCOME-TRANSITION-01`) & Settled-Modal WebGL Suspension**:
  - Under `BALANCED_60`, both `consumeRenderStep` and `startVictoryModalFx` check the live instantaneous `rAF` interval (`< 10.5ms`, `> ~95Hz`) and skip `< 12.5ms` sub-frames (accumulating exact `dtSec`) so `120Hz` drags lock cleanly to `60fps` without ever dropping jittered frames on `60Hz` displays, while capping WebGL DPR at `1.75` (`2.5` in `ULTRA_120`), burst counts (`36` vs `60`), max active particles (`384` vs `1024`), path traces (`3` vs `5`), and `#victory-fx-canvas` DPR (`1.75` vs `2`).
  - **Board-to-Dialog Choreography & Final-Shred Crescendo (`OUTCOME-TRANSITION-01`, `FINAL-SHRED-CRESCENDO-01`)**: `recordLevelWin` / `recordLevelLoss` persist synchronously at `0ms` while board/dock input locks (`!levelOutcomeHandled`), pacing an overlapped visual modal cross-dissolve (`290ms` Victory shred beat so the `220ms` gate-roller clip-plane shred & celebratory bloom launch unobstructed before the card dissolves in; `220ms` settle beat on Out of Moves / Time's Up; `350ms` 3D crimson-amber shockwave + radial burst `spawnBombDetonationBurstImpl` on Bomb Exploded; `0ms` under `reducedMotion` or `frozenTimeSec`). When the clearing block wins the stage (`isBoardWon(board)`), `detectAutomaticShredEventsImpl` & `spawnStyledShredShockwavesAndGateFx` elevate all 5 particle themes strictly on physical 3D gate hardware (zero sourceless center rings: unified `1.82×` true-hue burst accented with ~25% Burnished Champagne-Ivory Gold `[1.0, 0.89, 0.56]` and wand-jet `1.28×` `soap_bubbles` air-cushion brake, translucent single-quad `u_materialStyle = 10` courtyard-plane (`z = 0.034..0.042`) atmospheric gate waves (`peakAlpha = 0.68 / 0.72` block-hued flourish up to `maxRadius = 1.74` on normal moves vs. a 3-ring staggered ripple cascade `maxRadius = 2.18 / 1.84 / 1.48` with traveling Burnished Gold shimmer `u_clipPlane.w` on final clears) tucked beneath blocks and shredder gates, `JAW_CORONA` forge flare on the winning gate roller + staggered sympathetic `JAW_CORONA` resonance pulses along unlocked perimeter gates, interleaved gilded `PLASMA_ARCS` on `lightning_bolts`, `4.4 rad` roller spin, `0.22s` recoil, elevated `z = 0.68 / 0.70` `REDUCED_MOTION_SEAL` crests under `reducedMotion`, and an 11-grain ascending xylophone + full harmonic overtone `playShred(..., true)`). `#dialog-victory` and `#dialog-failure` materialize via `360ms` multi-stop GPU-composited `outcomeDialogEnter` (`opacity: 0 -> 0.48 -> 0.86 -> 1`, `scale: 0.935 -> 1`, `translate: 0 14px -> 0`, `fill-mode: none`) and `420ms` `outcomeBackdropEnter` (`0` `backdrop-filter`, `0` CSS `filter: blur(...)`).
  - While an outcome modal is open, `buildRenderOptionsImpl` sets `fxDecayMul = 1.4` so residual airborne shred/bomb particles keep arcing visibly through the translucent cross-dissolving card along `1.0×` trajectories before dissolving cleanly; the instant `hasActiveVisualFxImpl` reaches `false` and `modalSettleFrames` drain, `consumeRenderStep` suspends 3D WebGL draws to `0fps` (`requestModalBackdropRefresh()` re-draws when settings change).
  - `#telemetry-readout` updates at `<= 5Hz` (`200ms`) only when text changes and displays `FPS: <renderFps> (<displayHz>Hz) | ...`.
